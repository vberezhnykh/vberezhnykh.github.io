Privacy policy

HermesBot is a personal, single-user automation operated by its owner, not a public service.

Google Drive access

The application requests drive.readonly to find and read the owner's existing Google Drive files and folders, and drive.file to create and manage files created or explicitly opened by this application. The owner uses this access to locate document folders, save selected files, create backups, and check uploads.

Data handling

The owner runs the automation on a private server. OAuth credentials are held on that server and are not published on this website. Files selected for upload go to the owner's Google Drive. This website does not publish or provide access to the owner's Drive content or backups. The application does not sell Google user data or offer it to third-party users.

Control and contact

The owner can revoke Google access at any time in their Google Account's third-party connections. Files already uploaded to Google Drive remain under the owner's control in that account. For questions, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
